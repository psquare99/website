# P² Philosophy v1.0

Status: Approved
Version: 1.0
Last Updated: 29 July 2026

---

# Mission

P² exists to document a life in progress.

It is a place where anyone can discover what I am building, what I am learning, what I am exploring, and what I hope to create next. Instead of presenting a finished version of myself, P² embraces the idea that growth is continuous.

---

# Vision

Create a digital home that feels calm, personal and timeless.

Visitors should leave feeling that they met a real human being—not just another developer, gamer or creator.

The website should continue growing for years while remaining simple and approachable.

---

# What P² Is

P² is:

- A digital home.
- A window into my present life.
- A collection of meaningful projects and memories.
- A place where curiosity is rewarded.
- A record of continuous growth.

---

# What P² Is Not

P² is NOT:

- A résumé.
- A showcase of achievements.
- A social media profile.
- A marketing website.
- A collection of everything I've ever done.

Only meaningful things deserve a place here.

---

# Core Principles

## 1. Human Before Creator

Visitors should first see a person.

Projects, games, books and journals exist because they are parts of a life—not the other way around.

---

## 2. Present Before Past

The homepage should always answer one question first:

> "What is Prateek doing right now?"

History matters, but the present always comes first.

---

## 3. Curiosity Over Attention

The website should invite exploration rather than compete for attention.

Nothing should feel loud, flashy or overwhelming.

Visitors should naturally discover more by following their own interests.

---

## 4. Calm Wins

Every design decision should make the website feel:

- Calm
- Cozy
- Honest
- Minimal

If a feature creates clutter without adding meaning, it doesn't belong.

---

## 5. Build Slowly

P² is expected to evolve for many years.

Features should only be added when they genuinely improve the experience.

Growth is intentional—not rushed.

---

# Design Philosophy

The homepage is a window.

Windows do not explain everything.

They simply allow someone to look inside.

Every page should feel like opening another room inside the same house.

Nothing should feel disconnected.

---

# Content Philosophy

Content should focus on meaningful things.

Examples include:

- Projects
- Journal
- Books
- Games
- Places
- Learning
- Music
- Current work

Content should never exist simply to increase quantity.

Every page must answer:

> "Why does this deserve to exist?"

---

# Navigation Philosophy

Navigation should follow curiosity.

Visitors choose which thread to follow.

The website should never force a particular path.

Every major section represents a different part of life.

---

# The Window Principle

The homepage is not a dashboard.

It is not a résumé.

It is not a portfolio.

It is a window into the present.

Someone should be able to spend thirty seconds on the homepage and feel that they have met a real person.

---

# The Thread Philosophy

Every major interest becomes a thread.

Examples:

- Projects
- Journal
- Reading
- Travel
- Gaming
- Music

Threads grow independently while remaining connected to the same story.

---

# Future Philosophy

The structure of P² should remain stable.

Projects will change.

Books will change.

Games will change.

Locations will change.

The philosophy should not.

The foundation should be strong enough that new experiences naturally find their place.

---

# Success

Five years from now, success is NOT measured by:

- Number of visitors
- Stars
- Followers
- Likes

Success is opening P² and seeing a meaningful collection of projects, memories, places and ideas that genuinely represent the journey.

---

# Closing Statement

P² should feel like standing inside a cozy mountain cabin during the rain.

You open a window.

Outside are mountains.

The view keeps changing with the seasons.

The window remains the same.
