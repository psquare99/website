# Development Principles

## 1. Foundations First

Prefer making foundational improvements early if they reduce future work.

## 2. Avoid Premature Generalization

Don't abstract until there is a real second use case.

## 3. Content Drives Design

The design should support the content, not the other way around.

## 4. Calm Over Clever

Choose clarity and restraint over flashy interactions.

## 5. Iterate in Small Steps

Make one meaningful change at a time, then evaluate it.
