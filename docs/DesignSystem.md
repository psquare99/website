The website should feel calm, personal and timeless.
Visitors should feel like they are stepping into my workspace rather than reading a resume.

# Experience

The website should feel like opening the door to a quiet mountain cabin.

There is no pressure to stay.
There is no pressure to click.

Visitors are simply invited to look around,
see what I'm building,
and continue their own journey whenever they wish.

Come in.

This is what I'm building.

Stay awhile if you'd like.
## Writing Style

Write less.

Say only what is necessary.

Allow curiosity to guide visitors through the website instead of asking for their attention.
